Intern — Lab for Investigations of Local Air Quality

Claremont, CA · Sep 2025 – Present

Problem Statement

Monitoring hyperlocal air quality is difficult with legacy sensor middleware and fragmented data pipelines. The lab needs reliable, real-time CO₂ and meteorological data collection infrastructure.

Methodology

Prototyped a CO₂ indicator with I2C interface to SCD41 sensor. Wrote BASH and IGOR scripts for Raspberry Pi and Windows devices to stream data to Firebase from PurpleAir and meteorological sensors on legacy middleware. Designed sensor enclosure in SolidWorks using edit-in-context assembly workflow.

Intern — Hybrid Systems Lab, UC Santa Cruz

Santa Cruz, CA · Aug 2023 – Aug 2025

Problem Statement

Standard motion planners are designed for smooth continuous systems and fail on hybrid dynamical systems — those with both continuous flow and discrete jumps (e.g. a quadcopter bouncing off walls, a ball bouncing on a floor). No tools existed in OMPL for this class of systems.

Proposed Solution

Implemented cHyRRT and cHySST — two novel motion planners based on RRT and SST respectively — extended to handle hybrid dynamical systems with resets. Validated on a collision-resilient quadcopter and bouncing ball model. Results published at IEEE CASE 2025.
